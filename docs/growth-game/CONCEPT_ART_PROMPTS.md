# Bible Friend 3D Concept Art Prompt Pack

## Shared identity lock
Use this paragraph unchanged in every generation:

> Preserve the exact Bible Friend mascot identity: extremely cute rounded chibi proportions, oversized head and tiny soft body, orange-to-coral hood that encloses the head and flows around the body, deep violet/purple robe body, warm cream round face, simple dark oval eyes with large white catchlights, rosy cheeks, cheerful small mouth, cream star emblem centered on the chest. Child-friendly Christian mobile game aesthetic, soft toy-like 3D forms, no photorealism, no sharp threatening shapes, no text or logos, clean readable silhouette, consistent character proportions across every sheet.

Create a modular equipment concept sheet fitted to the Bible Friend mascot. Include six detachable pieces inspired by Ephesians 6: truth belt, righteousness breastplate, shoes of readiness/peace, faith shield, salvation helmet, Spirit sword representing the Word of God. All pieces must be rounded, toy-like, non-threatening, warm and luminous. Preserve orange/coral + violet identity. Show full equipped character plus separated equipment callouts. Avoid Roman-historical literalism; favor timeless child-friendly biblical fantasy motifs: stars, warm gold, ivory, violet, gentle sky-blue light.

## Sheet 10 — Wilderness encounter
Create a child-friendly biblical wilderness game environment: warm sand, rounded rocks, distant mountains, soft sunset-to-blue sky, a small path and glowing Scripture light markers. The “temptation/fear” enemy should be represented as a stylized smoky shadow with expressive but non-horror eyes, no demonic gore. Show the mascot using the faith shield and a glowing Word-of-God sword made of light. The emotional tone is courage and hope, not fear.

## Modeling acceptance criteria
- One canonical body and skeleton supports all equipment.
- 5k–15k triangles for base mascot.
- Equipment is separate GLB-compatible meshes with named attachment slots.
- Prefer flat/vertex colors or one 1K atlas; maximum two 1K atlases for base character.
- Animations: idle, happy, sleepy, read, pray, nourish/eat-light, walk, run, shield block, Scripture declaration, celebrate, comfort.
- No generated readable Bible text baked into textures; verses stay in UI for accuracy/localization.
